Before you order

STUDIO FAQ

Can I upload my own pattern or artwork?
Not yet — the Tee Studio only works with prints built in our Print Lab or saved from it. If you have your own artwork you'd like printed, get in touch and we'll see what we can do.
How long does a custom piece take?
Every custom order is cut and sewn after you buy — allow 7–10 working days before it ships. There's no warehouse stock to speed this up; that's what makes it yours alone.
Can I change my design after ordering?
Once an order is placed, cutting starts quickly, so changes can't be guaranteed. If you need to adjust something, contact us as soon as possible and we'll do what we can.
Ordering more than a few pieces?
For team or event orders, the Crew & Bulk page has volume pricing and a quote calculator built for exactly that.
What if the placement I want isn't listed?
The Studio covers chest, diagonal sweep, side stripe, pocket, both sleeves and all-over. If you need something outside that list, message us — we take on custom placements case by case.
